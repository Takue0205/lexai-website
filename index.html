The whole workflow

One spine, from the question to the work product.

  1. 1

    Matter

    Open a matter under a client. Every question, authority, document, and memo lives under it.

  2. 2

    Question

    State the legal question in your own words and set the position you are testing.

  3. 3

    Research

    Search Juta via the local connector, the browser extension, or the mobile paste panel — results come back into the matter.

  4. 4

    Authorities

    Save the cases that matter, each assessed for relevance against your question.

  5. 5

    Analysis

    AI Counsel reasons over saved authorities and documents you marked as context — with gaps stated plainly.

  6. 6

    Work product

    Build arguments, check citations, track events, and generate a research memo — every proposition traceable.

Research, Work, Documents, and AI Counsel tabs keep each matter's Overview, authorities, arguments, citations, timeline, and memos together.

What you get

Built for the work, not the demo.

Matters, clients, authorities

Dashboard, Clients, Matters, Authorities, and Settings — plus a private Library. Every question, note, document, and memo sits under the matter it belongs to.

Juta research, in place

Desktop connector with a managed browser session (no Juta credentials stored), a browser extension for one-click saves, and a mobile paste panel with clipboard read and AI relevance analysis.

Relevance you can trust

Each result is assessed against your actual legal question — Direct authority, Relevant, Potentially relevant, Mention only — with reasons, never an unexplained score.

AI Counsel, grounded

Grounded in saved authorities and documents you mark as context by default. Broader modes clearly label unverified guidance, and citations are checked against saved case text.

Work product that holds up

Draft our-side and opposing arguments, verify citations, log case events on a timeline, and generate a structured research memo with gaps stated as "not established".

Web and mobile

Lexai runs in the browser on computer and mobile, with an installable PWA and share-to-matter on phones. Sign in with password plus optional passkeys (Windows Hello, Touch ID, Android).

Grounded, not generated

Every answer points back to a real authority.

By default Lexai only reasons over the authorities you have saved to the matter and the documents you marked as context. When a memo makes a claim, the citation next to it is checked against the saved case text — gaps are stated as not established, and broader AI modes clearly label unverified guidance.
